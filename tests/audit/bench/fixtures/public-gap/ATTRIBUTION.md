# Gap-family fixtures (Phase 6): attribution

Synthetic documents (`gap-*`) are generated by `gen/gap_synth.py` from `specs-gap/*.json` (reportlab `invariant=1`, Pillow,
seeded noise, the vendored DejaVu Sans font); their ground truth is the generator's own drawing coordinates.

Each real `doc.pdf` below is the arXiv PDF, committed **unmodified** (sha256 in `manifest.json` and in the spec's
`pdf_sha256`). Each is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>), as recorded in arXiv's metadata (checked 2026-10-26). Ground truth
(`gt.json`) is agent-made with `gen/snap_gt.py` (`gt_source: "agent"`, `reviewed_by_human: false`); only the listed pages are
kind-scored, and only their figure, table and caption blocks are annotated.
